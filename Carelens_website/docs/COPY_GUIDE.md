# Copy Guide Draft

## Voice and tone
- Clear, calm, and direct.
- Premium but not exaggerated.
- Respectful and medically responsible.

## Approved framing
- "Coordenacao de cuidado"
- "Decisao clinica independente"
- "Condicoes de reembolso transparentes"

## Avoid
- Guaranteed prescription wording.
- Absolute efficacy claims.
- Ambiguous refund promises.

## Mandatory disclosure blocks
- Pricing and monthly renewal logic.
- Independent medical decision statement.
- Refund eligibility conditions and deduction note.
