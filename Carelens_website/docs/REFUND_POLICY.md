# Refund Policy Draft

## Principle
Levya keeps refund terms simple and visible before purchase.

## Current draft terms
- Monthly fee charged: BRL 1700.
- Refund can apply when there is no tirzepatide prescription outcome.
- Refund can also apply if patient chooses to buy outside partner pharmacies.
- Medical appointment amount is deducted from refunded value.

## Draft formula
- `refund_amount = monthly_subscription - appointment_deduction`
- Current draft appointment deduction in code: BRL 450.

## Required legal checks before production
- Confirm final appointment deduction amount.
- Confirm cancellation windows and timing.
- Confirm chargeback and dispute language.
- Confirm consumer-protection obligations for target jurisdictions.
