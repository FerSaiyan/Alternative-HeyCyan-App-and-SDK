# Levya Product Requirements Draft

## Mission
Deliver a clear digital path that coordinates patient onboarding, partner doctor consultation, and optional partner pharmacy purchasing.

## Core audience
- Adults seeking medically supervised access pathways.
- Users who need simple process clarity and refund transparency.

## Product goals
- Convert qualified visitors into paid subscribers.
- Minimize drop-off between payment and appointment scheduling.
- Reduce support volume by making refund conditions explicit.

## Primary pages
- Marketing home
- Onboarding (short)
- Checkout
- Scheduling
- Account status dashboard
- Admin operations dashboard

## Success metrics (v1)
- Landing to checkout conversion
- Checkout completion rate
- Payment to schedule completion rate
- Refund dispute rate
- Support tickets per 100 subscriptions
