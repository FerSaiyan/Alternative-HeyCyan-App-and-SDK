# Business Rules

## Commercial model
- Levya subscription price is BRL 1700 per month.
- Payment happens before appointment scheduling.

## Clinical independence
- Partner doctors decide prescription based on medical judgment only.
- Platform does not guarantee tirzepatide prescription.

## Pharmacy selection
- If prescribed, patient may purchase from partner pharmacy or any other pharmacy.

## Refund eligibility baseline
- Eligible when tirzepatide is not prescribed.
- Eligible when patient chooses to buy outside partner pharmacy network.
- Appointment cost is deducted from refund amount.

## Operational constraints
- Display rules in landing, checkout, and post-purchase communications.
- Persist timestamps for payment, consultation, decision, and refund actions.
